1. Appointment Ecosystem
    - Appointment booking at appointments page.
    - Appointment booked, RoomId created and sent to email and sms
    - Doctor is provided patient's medical history
    - Appointment takes places on online video call
    - Patient-Doctor consultation through offline chatting if needed
    - Doctor's genrates digital prescription according to patient

2. Analysis
    - Patient/doctor can upload reports (ie xray, ecg) to perform analysis
    - This contains XRAY, ECG, RETINOPATHY, ALZIEMERS, PET and SKIN disease analysis
    - Analysis is stored in patient's medical history

3. Emergency
    - Patient can locate nearest hospitals and medical within a specified radius in case of emergency
    - IVR to make a patient-reception communication for emergency

4. Additional Features
    - Every information is sent to email and sms
    - Voice assistant to make ease in using app/website
    - Rag Chatbot which helps to navigate through app/website and also helps in providing needed information to the user.
